CHAPTER 02

Haqeeqat

Phir ek din...

Woh ho jata hai jiske baare mein tumne kabhi seriously socha hi nahi tha.

Jis cheez ko tum hamesha "agar hua bhi to dekh lenge" keh kar side par rakh dete the...

Wohi tumhare saamne khari hoti hai.

Aur us waqt sabse ajeeb baat ye nahi hoti ke cheez buri hai.

Ajeeb baat ye hoti hai ke tum uske liye mentally ready hi nahi hote.

Tumhare paas koi plan nahi hota.

Koi jawab nahi hota.

Bas ek lambi si khamoshi hoti hai.

"Ye sach mein ho gaya?"

Jab "Sab Acha Hoga" Kaam Nahi Karta

Hum aksar khud ko tasalli dete hain.

"Result acha aa jayega."

"Kaam ho jayega."

"Log samajh jayenge."

"Dost saath rahenge."

"Ghar walay meri baat samajh lenge."

"Main ye kar lunga."

Aur ye sab kehna galat nahi. Umeed zaroori hai.

Lekin problem tab shuru hoti hai jab umeed ke saath hum possibility ko hi maar dete hain.

Hum ye sochna band kar dete hain:

"Agar aisa na hua to?"

Kyuki humein lagta hai ye sochna negative hona hai.

Nahi.

Kabhi kabhi "agar aisa na hua to?" poochna pessimism nahi hota. Ye preparation hoti hai.

Reality Warning Nahi Deti

Exam ka result tumse permission nahi leta.

Kisi opportunity ka reject hona tumse nahi poochta ke "tum ready ho?"

Koi dost badal jaye to wo calendar de kar nahi jata.

Ghar mein koi decision tumhari expectations ke against ho to zindagi ye nahi kehti:

"Thoda time le lo, phir accept karna."

Reality bas aa jati hai.

Aur jab aati hai...

To tumhari sari imaginary conversations ek dum bekaar lagne lagti hain.

Tumne socha tha:

"Main ye bolunga."

"Wo ye jawab denge."

"Phir sab theek ho jayega."

Lekin reality mein kisi ne tumhari script follow hi nahi ki.

Ek Result

Socho...

Tumne poora semester ek hi assumption ke saath guzara:

"Ho jayega."

Kabhi seriously nahi socha ke agar marks expected se kam aaye to kya karna hai.

Result ka din aata hai.

Screen khulti hai.

Number tumhari expectation se neeche hota hai.

Bas.

Duniya khatam nahi hui.

Suraj bhi nikla hua hai.

Ghar mein log apne kaam kar rahe hain.

Phone par notifications aa rahi hain.

Lekin tum kuch seconds ke liye apni jagah se hil nahi paate.

Kyuki problem sirf marks nahi hain.

Problem ye hai ke tumne apne dimagh mein ek hi future banaya tha.

Aur ab woh future toot gaya hai.

Tumhare paas doosra version tha hi nahi.

Haqeeqat kabhi kabhi humein dukh isliye nahi deti ke woh bohot buri hoti hai.

Balki isliye ke humne uske liye jagah hi nahi banayi hoti.

"Mujhe Laga Tha..."

Ye teen lafz bohot kuch keh dete hain.

"Mujhe laga tha sab theek ho jayega."

"Mujhe laga tha wo samjhenge."

"Mujhe laga tha ye opportunity mil jayegi."

"Mujhe laga tha hum sab aise hi rahenge."

"Mujhe laga tha main easily handle kar lunga."

Mujhe laga tha.

Kabhi kabhi dard reality se kam aur apni expectation se zyada hota hai.

Kyuki reality ne sirf ek cheez badli hoti hai.

Lekin tumne apne dimagh mein us reality ke around poori duniya bana rakhi hoti hai.

Ek plan.

Ek future.

Ek expectation.

Ek "phir".

Aur jab asli zindagi us imaginary duniya mein fit nahi hoti...

To sab kuch ajeeb lagne lagta hai.

Kuch Log Chale Nahi Jaate...

Bas Badal Jaate Hain.

Ye bhi ek haqeeqat hai jise hum accept karna nahi chahte.

Kabhi koi dost pehle har baat batata tha.

Phir replies chhote hone lagte hain.

Phir conversations kam.

Phir excuses zyada.

Aur ek din tum realize karte ho:

"Hum ab waise nahi rahe."

Koi fight nahi hui. Koi dramatic ending nahi.

Bas waqt ke saath ek distance aa gaya.

Aur tum sochte rehte ho:

"Shayad busy hoga."

"Shayad mood nahi hoga."

"Shayad kal pehle jaisa ho jayega."

Ye "shayad" bhi kabhi kabhi khushfehmi ka doosra naam hota hai.

Kyuki kuch cheezein wapas pehle jaisi nahi hotin.

Aur iska matlab ye nahi ke tumhari zindagi khatam ho gayi.

Bas ek chapter apni jagah badal raha hota hai.

Ghar Mein Bhi

Kabhi tum poori koshish karte ho apni baat samjhane ki.

Tum explain karte ho.

Phir dobara explain karte ho.

Phir examples dete ho.

Phir chup ho jaate ho.

Aur saamne wala phir bhi nahi samajhta.

Har insaan tumhari feelings ko tumhari tarah feel nahi kar sakta.

Hum chahte hain ke jo hum feel kar rahe hain saamne wala bina explain kiye samajh jaye.

Lekin log mind read nahi karte.

Kabhi kabhi unki apni problems hoti hain. Kabhi unka perspective different hota hai.

Aur kabhi... bas woh samajh nahi paate.

Ye accept karna mushkil hai.

Lekin har baar misunderstanding ka matlab ye nahi hota ke tum galat ho.

Aur har baar saamne wala na samjhe to tumhe apni feelings ko jhoota bhi nahi banana.

Haqeeqat Ka Sabse Mushkil Hissa

Haqeeqat ye nahi kehti:

"Tumhari expectations galat thi."

Wo bas itna kehti hai:

"Zindagi tumhari expectations ke mutabiq chalne ki zimmedar nahi."

Aur ye line sunne mein sakht hai.

Lekin shayad isi mein azaadi bhi hai.

Kyuki jis din tum samajh jaate ho ke har cheez tumhare control mein nahi...

Usi din tum control karne ki koshish kam kar dete ho.

Tum plan banate ho. Lekin plan ko zindagi nahi samajhte.

Tum umeed rakhte ho. Lekin umeed ko guarantee nahi samajhte.

Tum kisi par trust karte ho. Lekin apni poori duniya uske haath mein nahi dete.

"Mujhe umeed hai sab acha hoga. Aur agar acha nahi hua... to main dobara sochunga."

Ye maturity hai.

Aankhon Ke Saamne

Kabhi kabhi haqeeqat itni simple hoti hai ke hum usay accept hi nahi karna chahte.

Tum saamne dekh rahe hote ho.

Sab clear hota hai.

Phir bhi dimagh kehta hai:

"Nahi."

"Shayad main galat samajh raha hun."

"Shayad kal sab theek ho jayega."

"Shayad kuch change ho jaye."

Hum evidence ke saamne bhi apni imagination ko choose kar lete hain.

Kyuki imagination comfortable hoti hai.

Reality uncomfortable.

Lekin comfort aur truth hamesha ek hi jagah nahi rehte.

Kabhi kabhi sach ko accept karna khud ko todna nahi hota.

Khud ko jhoot se bachana hota hai.

Ek Chhota Sa Kamra

Raat ko tum bed par lete ho.

Phone side par.

Room dark.

Aur din bhar jo cheezein tum ignore karte rahe... wo sab ek ek karke yaad aati hain.

Wo result.

Wo conversation.

Wo missed opportunity.

Wo decision.

Wo sentence jo tumne suna aur pretend kiya ke farq nahi pada.

Tum ceiling dekhte ho.

Aur pehli baar khud se ye nahi kehte:

"Sab theek ho jayega."

Tum bas kehte ho:

"Haan... ye hua hai."

Sirf itna.

Aur ajeeb baat ye hai...

Kabhi kabhi acceptance solution nahi hota. Lekin solution ki taraf pehla sachcha qadam zaroor hota hai.

Haqeeqat Ko Accept Karna Haar Nahi

Hum acceptance ko haar samajhte hain.

Humein lagta hai:

"Agar maine maan liya ke cheez mere hisaab se nahi hui, to main weak ho gaya."

Nahi.

Acceptance ka matlab ye nahi ke tum agree karte ho.

Acceptance ka matlab sirf itna hai:

"Jo ho chuka hai, main usay jhutla nahi raha."

Tum phir bhi disagree kar sakte ho.

Tum phir bhi confuse ho sakte ho.

Tum phir bhi time le sakte ho.

Lekin tum reality se bhaag nahi rahe.

Aur jab bhaagna band hota hai...

Tab insaan pehli baar aage dekhna shuru karta hai.

Shayad Yahi Sabse Badi Haqeeqat Hai

Hum sab kuch control nahi kar sakte.

Hum ye control nahi kar sakte ke kaun badlega.

Kaun rukega.

Kaun samjhega.

Kaun reject karega.

Kaun disappoint karega.

Kaunsa result aayega.

Kaunsi opportunity milegi.

Zindagi har baar hamari planning follow nahi karegi.

Aur ye buri baat nahi. Ye bas zindagi hai.

Tumhara kaam har cheez ko apne mutabiq banana nahi.

Tumhara kaam ye seekhna hai ke jab cheezein tumhare mutabiq na hon... to tum kya karoge.

Aur yahin se ek naya sawaal paida hota hai.

Haqeeqat saamne aa gayi.

Jo hona tha, ho gaya.

Jo badalna tha, badal gaya.

Ab?
Ab kya?

Shayad isi sawaal se asal kahani shuru hoti hai.

Kyuki haqeeqat ko dekh lena ek baat hai...
aur uske baad kharay rehna doosri.
