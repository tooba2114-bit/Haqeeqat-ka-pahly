CHAPTER 01

Khushfehmi

Hum sab apne andar ek chhoti si duniya banate hain.

Aisi duniya jahan sab kuch waisa hota hai jaisa hum chahte hain.

Jahan plans cancel nahi hote.
Jahan log badalte nahi.
Jahan mehnat ka result zaroor milta hai.
Jahan apne log hamesha apne rehte hain.
Jahan jo socha hai, woh ho hi jayega.

Aur jab kabhi koi humse poochta hai,

“Agar aisa na hua to?”

Hum foran kehte hain,

“Dekhenge.”

Phir khud ko tasalli dete hain.

“Nahi hoga.”
“Sab acha hoga.”
“Mere saath aisa thori hoga.”
“Jab hoga tab dekhenge.”

Shayad isi ko hum khushfehmi kehte hain.

Khushfehmi hamesha koi bohot bari jhooti umeed nahi hoti.

Kabhi kabhi khushfehmi sirf ek chhota sa jumla hota hai jo hum roz khud se bolte hain:

“Sab theek ho jayega.”

Aur phir us jumle par itna yaqeen kar lete hain ke agar sab theek na ho...

to humein sirf problem ka saamna nahi karna padta.

Humein apni soch ke tootne ka bhi saamna karna padta hai.

Hum sirf acha sochna chahte hain

Humein bachpan se sikhaya jata hai:

Positive socho.
Achay ki umeed rakho.
Bura mat socho.

Aur shayad ye sab sahi bhi hai.

Har waqt bura sochna insaan ko thaka deta hai.

Lekin kabhi kabhi hum positivity aur khushfehmi ke darmiyan ka farq bhool jaate hain.

Positive sochna ye hai:

“Mujhe umeed hai sab acha hoga, lekin agar mushkil aayi to main usay handle karne ki koshish karunga.”

Khushfehmi ye hai:

“Mushkil aayegi hi nahi, isliye mujhe sochne ki zaroorat nahi.”

Farq bohot chhota lagta hai.

Lekin zindagi mein yehi chhota sa farq bohot kuch badal deta hai.

Hum aksar worst-case scenario sochne se darte hain. Humein lagta hai ke agar humne bura soch liya to shayad woh sach ho jayega.

Isliye hum aankhein band kar lete hain.

Jaise sarak par aankhein band karne se gaariyan ruk jaati hon.

Jaise result ke baare mein na sochne se result badal jayega.

Jaise kisi problem ko ignore karne se problem khatam ho jayegi.

Lekin waqt kabhi nahi rukta.

Reality bhi nahi.

“Ye mere saath nahi hoga.”

Kitni ajeeb line hai.

Hum sab ne kabhi na kabhi kahi hai.

Kisi aur ke saath kuch hota hai to hum uski kahani sunte hain. Thori der afsos karte hain. Phir apni zindagi ki taraf dekh kar sochte hain:

“Mere saath to aisa nahi hoga.”

Hum har kahani mein khud ke liye ek exception dhoond lete hain.

Aur shayad isi liye jab zindagi kabhi humein bhi wahi chapter khol kar dikha deti hai...

“Lekin ye to mere saath nahi hona tha.”

Reality jawab nahi deti.

Bas saamne khari rehti hai.

Exam se pehle

Tum exam se ek din pehle kitab kholte ho.

Pehle chapter ko dekh kar kehte ho, “Ye to easy hai.”

Doosre ko dekhte ho. “Ye bhi ho jayega.”

Phir phone haath mein aa jata hai. “Thori der baad parh lunga.”

Raat hoti hai.

Tum khud ko kehte ho, “Abhi to poori raat pari hai.”

Phir subah hoti hai.

Aur achanak wohi kitab jo kal tak manageable lag rahi thi... bohot zyada badi lagne lagti hai.

Masla ye nahi tha ke exam mushkil tha.

Masla ye tha ke tumne kabhi honestly ye socha hi nahi tha:

“Agar meri preparation complete na hui to?”

Har cheez ka plan hota hai...

Bas “agar na hua” ka nahi.

Hum future ke liye plans banate hain. Career ka plan. Degree ka plan. Job ka plan. Travel ka plan. Paise ka plan.

Lekin aksar ek cheez plan mein nahi hoti:

“Agar plan fail ho gaya to?”

Hum destination decide kar lete hain. Alternative route nahi.

Hum ek darwaza dekh kar usi ko apna raasta samajh lete hain.

Phir jab woh darwaza band milta hai... humein lagta hai duniya khatam ho gayi.

Halanke duniya khatam nahi hui hoti. Sirf woh raasta band hua hota hai.

Masla girna nahi tha.
Masla ye tha ke humne kabhi socha hi nahi tha
ke hum gir bhi sakte hain.

“Koi baat nahi.”

Kabhi kabhi khushfehmi sirf future ke baare mein nahi hoti. Kabhi hum present ki reality se bhi bach rahe hote hain.

Kisi ke lafz dil ko lagte hain. Tum kuch nahi kehte. Bas muskura kar bol dete ho:

“Koi baat nahi.”

Kisi ne tumhari expectation puri nahi ki. “Koi baat nahi.”

Kisi ne tumhein disappoint kiya. “Busy hoga.”

Kisi ne tumhari feelings ko seriously nahi liya. “Shayad main hi zyada soch raha hun.”

Aur dheere dheere hum har cheez ko explain karne lagte hain. Dusron ke liye. Unke behaviour ke liye. Unki ghaltiyon ke liye.

Bas apne dil ke liye nahi.

Hum zakhm ko bandage nahi lagate.
Usay “choti si kharash” bol kar bhool jaate hain.

Kuch log bohot bolte hain

Har waqt. Har jagah. Har kisi ke saath.

Hansi mazaak. Stories. Random baatein. Har khamoshi ko kisi na kisi baat se bhar dena.

Aur zaroori nahi ke har aisa insaan andar se udaas ho.

Lekin kabhi kabhi insaan baat karta rehta hai kyunki khamoshi usay apne andar ki awaaz suna deti hai.

Isliye woh baat badal deta hai. Topic badal deta hai. Hansi mein uda deta hai.

Kuch log kam bolte hain. Kuch bohot zyada.

Insaan isse bohot zyada complicated hai.

Har muskurahat khushi ka saboot nahi hoti.
Aur har khamoshi gham ka.

Hum busy bhi ho jaate hain

Jab koi thought baar baar dimagh mein aaye... hum khud ko busy kar lete hain.

Phone, Assignments, Work, Friends, Videos, Scrolling. Kuch bhi.

Bas woh thought dobara saamne na aaye.

Lekin kabhi kabhi time nahi...

khud se milne se bach rahe hote hain.

Kyunki khud se milne ke liye khamoshi chahiye.

Khushfehmi ka sabse dangerous hissa

Khushfehmi ye nahi ke tumne acha socha.

Khushfehmi ye hai ke tumne sirf acha socha.

Tumne possibility ko possibility nahi samjha. Tumne umeed ko guarantee bana diya.

Aur jab guarantee toot gayi... tumhari umeed ke saath tumhara balance bhi toot gaya.

Isliye shayad humein negative nahi banna. Humein bas prepared hona hai.

“Agar bura hua to?”

Aur phir khud se poochna:

“Main kya karunga?”

Ye pessimism nahi.

Ye maturity hai.

Ek chhota sa “agar”

Zindagi shayad “agar” se bohot zyada badal sakti hai.

Agar plan fail ho gaya to?

Agar jis cheez ki umeed thi woh na mili to?

Agar kisi apne ka behaviour badal gaya to?

Agar mehnat ka result waisa na aaya to?

Agar tumhari chosen direction tumhare liye sahi na nikli to?

Agar tumhein dobara shuru karna pada to?

In sawaalon ka matlab ye nahi ke tum apne liye bura expect karo.

Inka matlab sirf itna hai:

Tum apni zindagi ki ek hi possible ending par depend nahi kar rahe.

Hum reality se kyun darte hain?

Kyunki reality mein control kam hota hai.

Khushfehmi mein sab kuch hamari marzi ka hota hai.

Wahan hum keh sakte hain: “Sab acha hoga.”

Reality kehti hai: “Dekhte hain.”

Khushfehmi mein hum kahani likhte hain. Reality mein kahani humein surprise karti hai.

Aur surprise hamesha bura nahi hota.

Kabhi reality humari umeed se bhi achi hoti hai.

Khushfehmi ka ghar bohot khoobsurat hota hai.

Wahan khidkiyon se hamesha roshni aati hai. Wahan future hamesha perfect lagta hai. Wahan har “kal” acha hota hai.

Lekin problem ye hai...

us ghar ki deewarein haqeeqat ki nahi hoti.

Aur jab reality ka pehla pathar un par lagta hai... humein lagta hai ghar toot gaya.

Shayad ghar nahi toota hota. Shayad sirf woh tasveer tooti hoti hai jo humne apne dimagh mein bana rakhi thi.

Aur tasveer tootne ke baad...

pehli baar hum asli cheez dekhte hain.

Seedhi.
Bina tasalli ke.

Haqeeqat.

Aur shayad isi liye... hum khushfehmi ko itna pasand karte hain.

Kyunki khushfehmi mein sawaal kam hote hain. Reality mein bohot.

“Agar meri sochi hui zindagi aur meri asli zindagi ek jaisi na hui...
to kya main phir bhi jeena seekh sakta hun?”

Shayad jawab abhi nahi pata.
