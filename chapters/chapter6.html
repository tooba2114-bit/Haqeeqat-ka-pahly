CHAPTER 06

Raasta

Dhund hamesha nahi rehti.

Kabhi hawa chalti hai, kabhi roshni badalti hai, aur kabhi bas hum khud itna chal chuke hote hain ke cheezein pehle se zyada saaf nazar aane lagti hain.

Ek waqt tha jab humein nahi pata tha ke jaana kahan hai.

Ab shayad sab kuch pata nahi. Lekin ek direction nazar aati hai.

Aur kabhi kabhi direction hi kaafi hoti hai.

Raasta Milta Nahi

Hum aksar sochte hain ke raasta kahin pehle se bana hua hoga. Bas humein usay dhoondna hai.

Jaise zindagi ke har sawal ka ek perfect answer ho, har decision ka ek perfect waqt ho, aur har insan ke liye ek fixed direction likhi ho.

Lekin shayad raasta milta nahi.

Shayad raasta banta hai.

Har decision se. Har mistake se. Har "haan" se. Har "nahi" se. Har us qadam se jo humne darr ke bawajood uthaya.

Peeche mud kar dekho to kuch cheezein random lagti hain.

Lekin wahi random cheezein mil kar kabhi kabhi ek raasta bana deti hain.

"Raasta aksar wahan se shuru hota hai jahan hum intezar karna band kar dete hain."

Chhota Qadam

Zindagi ko dobara banana hamesha kisi bohot bade decision se start nahi hota.

Kabhi ek chhota sa kaam.

Ek nayi skill seekhna. Ek purani aadat chhorna. Apna routine dobara banana. Kisi opportunity ke liye apply karna. Kisi kitab ka pehla page kholna. Kisi cheez ko dobara try karna.

Ye sab dekhne mein chhoti cheezein hain.

Lekin zindagi aksar chhoti cheezon se hi direction leti hai.

Humein har waqt apni poori zindagi fix karne ki zaroorat nahi.

Kabhi sirf aaj ko sambhalna bhi ek qadam hota hai.

Apni Raftaar

Raasta milne ke baad bhi ek problem rehti hai: hum doosron ki speed dekhte rehte hain.

Koi humse pehle pohanch gaya. Koi humse kam waqt mein woh kar gaya jo hum abhi seekh rahe hain.

Aur phir hum apni raftaar ko apni worth samajhne lagte hain.

Agar main slow hoon, to shayad main capable nahi.

Agar mujhe zyada waqt lag raha hai, to shayad main peeche hoon.

Lekin har raasta race nahi hota.

Kuch log seedha chalte hain. Kuch log ruk kar seekhte hain. Kuch log direction badalte hain. Kuch log dobara shuru karte hain.

Aur in sab ka matlab ye nahi ke kisi ek ki zindagi galat hai.

Har kisi ka waqt ek jaisa nahi hota.

Dobara Bharosa

Sab se mushkil cheez kabhi kabhi doosron par bharosa karna nahi hoti.

Khud par dobara bharosa karna hota hai.

Khaas taur par tab, jab tumse kuch decisions galat hue hon.

Jab tumne kisi cheez ko sahi samjha aur woh sahi nahi nikli.

Jab tumhari expectation reality se match nahi hui.

Jab tumne kisi par yaqeen kiya aur tumhein apni judgement par hi doubt hone laga.

Phir agla decision lene se pehle ek awaaz andar se kehti hai:

"Agar phir galat hua to?"

Lekin har galti ka matlab ye nahi ke tum future mein har baar galat hi choose karoge.

Experience ka matlab ye nahi ke ab koi risk nahi lena.

Experience ka matlab hai: ab tum pehle se zyada samajh kar qadam uthate ho.

Har Dar Ko Sunna Zaroori Nahi

Dar kabhi kabhi useful hota hai. Woh humein rok kar sochne par majboor karta hai.

Lekin har dar sach nahi hota.

Kuch dar sirf purani yaadon ki awaaz hote hain.

Ek baar kuch bura ho jaye to hum assume kar lete hain ke ab har baar bura hi hoga.

Lekin past warning ho sakta hai, future ka faisla nahi.

Agar ek baar trust toot gaya, iska matlab ye nahi ke duniya mein har insan same hai.

Agar ek plan fail hua, iska matlab ye nahi ke har naya plan bhi fail hoga.

Agar ek jagah tumhare liye sahi nahi thi, iska matlab ye nahi ke tumhare liye koi jagah hai hi nahi.

Har bura experience ek rule nahi hota.

Khud Se Ek Sawal

Shayad raasta dhoondte waqt hum sab se important sawal hi nahi poochte.

Hum poochte hain: "Log kya kar rahe hain?"

"Mujhe kya karna chahiye?"

"Kaunsa option successful hoga?"

Lekin shayad kabhi ye bhi poochna chahiye:

"Main asal mein chahta kya hoon?"

Na woh jo sab keh rahe hain. Na woh jo sirf impressive lagta hai. Na woh jo comparison mein acha nazar aata hai.

Bas woh jo tumhare liye meaningful hai.

Kyunki doosron ke map se apni zindagi ka raasta nahi banaya ja sakta.

Raasta Hamesha Seedha Nahi Hota

Kabhi hum sochte hain ke agar direction sahi hai to sab kuch smooth hona chahiye.

Lekin sahi raasta bhi mushkil ho sakta hai.

Us mein delay ho sakta hai. Confusion aa sakti hai. Thakan aa sakti hai. Kabhi wapas mudna pad sakta hai.

Kabhi humein lagega ke hum phir se wahi aa gaye hain.

Lekin wapas kisi point par aana hamesha wahi insan ban kar aana nahi hota.

Tum ab woh nahi ho jo pehle the.

Tumne dekha hai. Samjha hai. Kuch khoya hai. Kuch seekha hai.

Isliye agar tum wahi sawal dobara face kar rahe ho, tumhare paas ab pehle se zyada jawab hain.

Apni Zindagi Ko Dobara Likho

Kabhi kabhi hum apni purani story ko itna seriously le lete hain ke bhool jaate hain: story abhi khatam nahi hui.

Jo pehle hua, woh important hai.

Lekin woh final chapter nahi.

Tumhare saath jo hua, woh tumhari kahani ka hissa hai.

Tumhari poori pehchan nahi.

Ek galat decision tumhari poori personality nahi. Ek difficult year tumhari poori zindagi nahi. Ek failure tumhara final result nahi.

"Past ko mita nahi sakte. Lekin uske baad kya likhna hai, us mein tumhara hissa zaroor hota hai."

Manzil Se Pehle

Shayad hum manzil ko bohot zyada importance dete hain.

Itni ke raaste ko dekhna hi bhool jaate hain.

Hum kehte hain: "Jab main wahan pohanchunga tab..."

Jab degree complete hogi. Jab job milegi. Jab paisa hoga. Jab sab samajhne lagenge. Jab life settle ho jayegi.

Lekin "jab" ke peeche zindagi ka bohot saara hissa chup jata hai.

Ho sakta hai manzil important ho. Lekin tum jis insan mein us manzil tak pohanchte hue badalte ho, woh bhi important hai.

Raasta sirf tumhein kahin le kar nahi jata.

Raasta tumhein banata bhi hai.

Ab shayad sab kuch clear nahi.

Lekin ab humein ye samajh aa gaya hai ke clarity ka intezar karte rehna zaroori nahi.

Kabhi qadam uthana padta hai, phir raasta khud thoda thoda saamne aata hai.

Shayad humari zindagi ka har mod pehle se likha hua nahi tha.

Shayad kuch mod humein khud banana the.

Aur shayad ab waqt hai ke hum sirf ye na poochein ke raasta kahan hai... balki ye bhi sochein ke hum kis taraf chalna chahte hain.

Kinara.
