CHAPTER 07

Kinara

Har safar ka matlab ye nahi hota ke hum kisi ek jagah pohanch kar ruk jayen.

Kabhi kabhi safar ka matlab sirf itna hota hai ke hum woh insan nahi rehte jo safar ke shuru mein the.

Hum badalte hain. Kuch cheezein peeche reh jaati hain. Kuch sawal khatam ho jaate hain. Kuch naye sawal paida hote hain.

Aur phir ek waqt aata hai jab humein mehsoos hota hai ke hum ab us jagah kharay hain jahan se peeche dekhna mumkin hai, magar wapas jaana zaroori nahi.

Shayad isi ehsaas ka naam Kinara hai.

Har Safar Ka Ek Kinara Hota Hai

Kinara hamesha khushi ki jagah nahi hota.

Kabhi kinare par kharay ho kar hum sirf thakawat mehsoos karte hain.

Itna kuch guzra hota hai ke khushi se zyada sukoon ajeeb lagta hai.

Hum sochte hain: "Bas ab aur kuch na badle."

Lekin shayad kinara kisi perfect ending ka naam nahi.

Ye sirf woh jagah hai jahan hum pehli baar apne guzray hue safar ko thoda door se dekh pate hain.

Aur jab hum door se dekhte hain, to kuch cheezein samajh aati hain jo safar ke beech mein kabhi samajh nahi aayi thin.

"Kuch raaste tab samajh aate hain jab hum un par chal chuke hote hain."

Jo Hona Tha

Zindagi mein bohot si cheezein humare control mein nahi hotin.

Hum apni mehnat control kar sakte hain, lekin har result nahi.

Hum apna rawayya control kar sakte hain, lekin har insan ka rawayya nahi.

Hum apni expectations samajh sakte hain, lekin doosron ke decisions nahi.

Aur shayad maturity ka ek hissa ye samajhna hai ke har cheez ko control karne ki koshish zaroori nahi hoti.

Kuch cheezein hoti hain jinhein bas accept karna padta hai.

Isliye nahi ke woh sahi thin.

Balki isliye ke woh ho chuki hain.

Acceptance ka matlab ye nahi ke jo hua woh theek tha. Acceptance ka matlab hai: jo hua, usay ab deny nahi karna.

Ab Har Cheez Se Darna Nahi

Ek waqt tha jab reality se takra kar hum har cheez se bachna chahte the.

Kisi par trust na karo. Zyada expect na karo. Zyada attach na ho. Zyada plan na banao. Zyada khush na ho.

Jaise agar hum kam umeed rakhenge to kam hurt honge.

Lekin ye bhi ek tarah ki zindagi nahi.

Har cheez se bachne ki koshish mein hum un cheezon se bhi door ho sakte hain jo achi ho sakti hain.

Past humein cautious bana sakta hai, lekin permanently closed nahi.

Humein har naye insan ko purane experience ke scale par nahi tolna.

Har naye chance ko purani failure ki wajah se reject nahi karna.

Hoshiyar rehna aur khauf mein jeena, dono ek cheez nahi.

Khushfehmi Se Haqeeqat Tak

Shayad ab hum wapas us pehle point par aa gaye hain jahan se ye safar shuru hua tha.

Khushfehmi.

Woh duniya jo hum apne zehan mein bana lete hain. Jahan sab kuch waise hi hota hai jaise humne socha hota hai.

Phir haqeeqat aati hai.

Phir sawal: "Ab kya?"

Phir khalipan.

Phir dhund.

Phir raasta.

Aur phir... kinara.

Lekin is poore safar ke baad ek cheez badal chuki hoti hai.

Ab hum jaante hain ke har umeed guarantee nahi hoti.

Har plan final nahi hota.

Har insan hamesha same nahi rehta.

Aur har mushkil ka matlab zindagi khatam hona nahi.

Umeed, Lekin Aankhein Khuli Hui

Shayad kitab ka sab se important lesson positive rehna nahi.

Shayad lesson ye hai ke umeed rakho, lekin umeed ko haqeeqat mat samjho.

Kisi cheez ke acha hone ki umeed rakho, lekin agar waisa na ho to khud ko sambhalne ki himmat bhi rakho.

Kisi insan par trust karo, lekin apni poori duniya sirf us trust ke hawale mat karo.

Koi plan banao, lekin alternate raasta sochne mein sharm mehsoos mat karo.

Khush raho, lekin apne dukh ko deny karne ki zaroorat nahi.

"Umeed rakho. Bas itni nahi ke haqeeqat ko dekhna mushkil ho jaye."

Kuch Log Samajh Nahi Paate

Safar ke end par ek aur cheez samajh aati hai: har koi tumhein samjhega nahi.

Tum kitna bhi explain kar lo, kuch log tumhari khamoshi ko attitude samjhenge.

Tumhari thakan ko laziness.

Tumhari boundaries ko ego.

Tumhari silence ko distance.

Aur kabhi kabhi tum jitna samjhana chahte ho, utna hi zyada thak jaate ho.

Phir ek waqt aata hai jab tum samajhte ho: har misunderstanding ko solve karna tumhari responsibility nahi.

Kuch jagah explanation zaroori hai.

Aur kuch jagah silence hi enough hai.

Khud Se Sulah

Shayad sab se important relationship woh hai jo insan apne aap se rakhta hai.

Hum aksar doosron ki galtiyan maaf kar dete hain, lekin apni purani mistakes ko saalon tak yaad rakhte hain.

"Mujhe aisa nahi karna chahiye tha."

"Mujhe pehle samajh jana chahiye tha."

"Maine us waqt kuch aur decision kyun nahi liya?"

Lekin us waqt jo tum jaante the, tumne usi knowledge ke saath decision liya tha.

Aaj ki samajh ko kal ke insan ke against use karna hamesha fair nahi hota.

Tumhe apne purane version se nafrat karne ki zaroorat nahi.

Usne bhi apni taraf se cheezein samajhne ki koshish ki thi.

Kabhi kabhi aage barhne ke liye kisi aur ko nahi, khud ko maaf karna padta hai.

Ab Wapas Wahi Nahi Hoga

Shayad sab se ajeeb feeling ye hoti hai ke tum theek ho jaate ho, lekin pehle jaise nahi.

Tum phir haste ho. Phir plans banate ho. Phir logon se baat karte ho. Phir kisi cheez ka intezar karte ho.

Lekin tumhare andar ek samajh aa chuki hoti hai jo pehle nahi thi.

Ab tum jaante ho ke kuch bhi permanent promise ke saath nahi aata.

Aur shayad ye knowledge zindagi ko boring nahi banati.

Ye usay real banati hai.

Kinare Par Khare Ho Kar

Ab jab hum peeche dekhte hain to khushfehmi buri nahi lagti.

Woh bas ek stage lagti hai.

Hum sab kisi na kisi waqt apne liye ek perfect duniya imagine karte hain.

Ye human hai.

Problem umeed karna nahi thi.

Problem ye thi ke humne umeed ko certainty bana diya tha.

Ab shayad hum umeed rakh sakte hain aur saath hi ye bhi maan sakte hain ke cheezein different ho sakti hain.

Aur agar different hui... to hum dobara sochenge.

Dobara chalenge.

Dobara banayenge.

Kyunki zindagi ka matlab ye nahi ke har cheez humari marzi ke mutabiq ho.

Shayad zindagi ka matlab ye hai ke jo milay, usay samjho.

Jo chala jaye, usay accept karo.

Jo toot jaye, us se seekho.

Aur jo naya aaye, usay purani kahani ki saza mat do.

To phir... kya haqeeqat se pehle wali duniya galat thi?

Shayad nahi.

Woh bas adhoori thi.

Us mein umeedein thin, lekin possibilities ka hisaab nahi tha.

Us mein plans thay, lekin alternate raaste nahi.

Us mein log thay, lekin unke badalne ki gunjaish nahi.

Phir haqeeqat aayi.

Kuch cheezein toot gayin. Kuch samajh aa gayin. Kuch log door ho gaye. Kuch sawal reh gaye.

Aur hum... hum bhi pehle jaise nahi rahe.

Shayad yehi zindagi hai — khushfehmi se haqeeqat tak, aur haqeeqat se khud tak ka safar.

Aur agar kal phir koi cheez humari soch ke mutabiq na hui...

to shayad is baar hum tootenge nahi.

Shayad is baar hum rukenge, dekhenge, samjhenge... aur phir chal denge.

Kyunki ab humein pata hai: haqeeqat se bachna mumkin nahi.

Lekin haqeeqat ke baad bhi zindagi mumkin hai.

Kinara.
