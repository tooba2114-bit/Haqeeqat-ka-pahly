CHAPTER 03

Ab Kya?

Haqeeqat saamne aa gayi thi. Jo socha tha, zaroori nahi tha ke waise hi hota. Jo log hamesha samajhne wale lagte thay, zaroori nahi tha ke har baar samajhte. Jo plans bohot mazboot lagte thay, zaroori nahi tha ke har plan apni manzil tak pohanchta.

Aur phir ek sawaal reh gaya tha.

Ab kya?

Ye sawaal chhota hai. Lekin kabhi kabhi poori zindagi se zyada bhaari lagta hai.

Kyunki jab tak hum kisi cheez ke hone ki umeed mein hote hain, tab tak humare paas kuch na kuch karne ko hota hai. Sochna hota hai. Plan banana hota hai. Intezaar karna hota hai. Khud ko samjhana hota hai ke sab theek ho jayega.

Lekin jab woh cheez ho nahi paati... ya bilkul us tarah nahi hoti jaisa humne socha tha, to achanak sab kuch ruk sa jata hai.

Kabhi kabhi problem ye nahi hoti ke hum haar gaye. Problem ye hoti hai ke humein samajh nahi aata ke haarne ke baad kharay kahan hona hai.

Jab Purana Plan Khatam Ho Jaye

Hum aksar apni zindagi ko ek seedhi line samajhte hain.

School ke baad college. College ke baad degree. Degree ke baad job. Job ke baad stability. Aur phir shayad woh sab kuch jo humne kabhi imagine kiya tha.

Har stage ke saath ek picture bani hoti hai. Hum sochte hain ke agla step kya hoga. Lekin bohot kam hum ye sochte hain:

Agar agla step hua hi nahi to?

Agar jis field ko choose kiya tha woh suit na kare? Agar jis opportunity ka intezaar tha woh na mile? Agar jis plan par itna bharosa tha woh beech mein ruk jaye?

Ye sawaal darane ke liye nahi hote. Ye sirf humein yaad dilate hain ke zindagi ek hi raaste ki mohtaj nahi.

Ek plan ka fail hona, zindagi ka fail hona nahi hota.

Lekin jab humne apni poori umeed ek hi plan ke saath baandh di ho, to us plan ke tootne par lagta hai jaise sab kuch toot gaya.

Shayad isi liye kabhi kabhi humein dobara shuru karne se pehle ye samajhna padta hai ke hum asal mein kya khona chuke hain.

Har Cheez Ko Theek Karna Zaroori Nahi

Haqeeqat accept karne ke baad ek aur galti hoti hai.

Hum foran sab kuch theek karna chahte hain.

Jo kharab hua, usay theek karo. Jo badal gaya, usay wapas pehle jaisa karo. Jo samajh nahi raha, usay samjhao. Jo hurt kar gaya, us se jawab lo. Jo plan fail hua, usay kisi bhi tarah complete karo.

Jaise zindagi mein har cheez ka solution hona chahiye. Aur agar solution na mile, to hum samajhte hain ke humne koshish kam ki hai.

Lekin har cheez repair nahi hoti. Kuch cheezein sirf accept hoti hain.

Har tooti hui cheez ko jorna zaroori nahi hota. Kuch cheezein humein sirf ye sikhane aati hain ke woh toot bhi sakti thi.

Is baat ko samajhna asaan nahi. Kyunki hum action ke aadhi hote hain. Humein lagta hai agar kuch bura hua hai to humein kuch karna chahiye.

Kabhi kabhi "kuch karna" ye hota hai ke hum thoda ruk jayein. Sochain. Samjhein. Aur phir decide karein ke kya bachaya ja sakta hai aur kya sirf chhorna padega.

Khud Ko Ilzaam Dena

Reality ke baad dimagh aksar peeche jana shuru kar deta hai.

"Kaash maine pehle samajh liya hota."

"Kaash maine ye decision na liya hota."

"Kaash maine us waqt kuch keh diya hota."

"Kaash main thoda aur prepare hota."

"Kaash..."

Ye chhota sa lafz kabhi kabhi bohot lambi raat ban jata hai.

Hum purani situation ko naye knowledge ke saath judge karne lagte hain. Aaj humein woh cheez pata hai jo us waqt nahi pata thi. Aaj humein woh signs nazar aate hain jo us waqt simple lag rahe thay.

Lekin us waqt humare paas sirf us waqt ki information thi.

Purani version of yourself ko aaj ki samajh se punish karna insaaf nahi.

Iska matlab ye nahi ke apni mistakes se kuch na seekho. Seekho. Zaroor seekho.

Lekin mistake aur identity mein farq rakho.

"Maine galat decision liya" aur "Main hi galat hun" ek baat nahi hai.

Log Tumhein Samajh Nahi Paayenge

Shayad reality accept karne ka ek hissa ye bhi hai ke tum har kisi ko apni reality samjha nahi sakte.

Tum kisi ko bata sakte ho ke tum hurt hue. Lekin tum usay majboor nahi kar sakte ke woh tumhari feelings ko bilkul usi tarah samjhe.

Tum apni side explain kar sakte ho. Examples de sakte ho. Apni khamoshi ka reason bata sakte ho. Lekin saamne wala phir bhi keh sakta hai:

"Mera wo matlab nahi tha."

Aur phir tumhare paas do raaste hote hain.

Ya to tum baar baar prove karte raho ke tumhara hurt valid hai... ya phir samajh jao ke har samajh kisi aur se lena zaroori nahi.

Har kisi ka tumhein samajhna zaroori nahi. Kabhi kabhi tumhara khud ko samajhna hi kaafi hota hai.

Ye loneliness nahi. Ye self-understanding hai.

"Koi Baat Nahi" Ke Baad

Humne bohot baar "koi baat nahi" kaha hota hai.

Kabhi isliye ke saamne wale ko bura na lage. Kabhi isliye ke baat barh na jaye. Kabhi isliye ke humein khud nahi pata hota ke apni feelings explain kaise karni hain.

Aur kabhi hum itni baar "koi baat nahi" keh dete hain ke aakhir mein khud ko bhi yaqeen dilane lagte hain ke waqai koi baat nahi thi.

Lekin har baar chup rehna maturity nahi hota. Aur har baar bol dena bhi strength nahi hoti.

Kabhi strength ye decide karna hoti hai ke kis baat ko kehna hai, kis baat ko chhorna hai, aur kis jagah distance rakhna hai.

Boundaries hamesha gusse se nahi banti. Kabhi kabhi woh sirf ek quiet decision hota hai:

"Main is cheez ko dobara apni zindagi mein itni jagah nahi dunga."

Har Baar Pehle Jaisa Nahi Hota

Kuch logon ke saath relation pehle jaisa nahi rehta. Kuch conversations kam ho jati hain. Kuch jagahen ajeeb lagne lagti hain. Kuch cheezein jo pehle khushi deti thi, unka matlab badal jata hai.

Aur hum aksar purani feeling ko wapas lane ki koshish karte hain.

"Pehle jaisa kyun nahi raha?"

Shayad kyunki hum bhi pehle jaise nahi rahe.

Reality sirf situations ko nahi badalti. Kabhi kabhi woh humari nazar bhi badal deti hai.

Jo cheez pehle normal lagti thi, ab noticeable lagti hai. Jo baat pehle ignore kar dete thay, ab uska weight samajh aata hai.

Kuch cheezein isliye nahi badalti ke duniya badal gayi hoti hai. Kabhi hum unhein pehli baar clearly dekh rahe hote hain.

Dobara Shuru Karna

"Ab kya?" ka matlab hamesha koi bada answer nahi hota.

Kabhi iska answer bohot simple hota hai.

Kal subah uthna. Apna routine wapas banana. Jo kaam ruk gaya tha usay dobara shuru karna. Jo decision pending tha usay dekhna. Apni priorities dobara likhna.

Zindagi aksar dramatic music ke saath restart nahi hoti.

Kabhi woh bas ek normal subah hoti hai. Alarm bajta hai. Tum uthte ho. Aur tumhein yaad aata hai ke jo hua, woh change nahi hoga.

Lekin tumhara aaj abhi tumhare paas hai.

Restart ka matlab ye nahi ke kuch hua hi nahi. Restart ka matlab hai: jo hua, uske baad bhi main aage chal raha hun.

Ab Har Cheez Se Darna Bhi Nahi

Reality samajhne ke baad ek aur extreme aa sakta hai.

Insaan har cheez se doubt karna shuru kar deta hai.

"Agar ye bhi fail ho gaya to?" "Agar ye person bhi badal gaya to?" "Agar ye plan bhi nahi chala to?"

Ye bhi wohi trap hai. Bas is baar khushfehmi ki jagah fear ne le li hoti hai.

Reality ka matlab ye nahi ke har buri possibility ko expect karo. Aur na hi ye ke kisi par trust na karo.

Matlab sirf itna hai: umeed rakho, lekin apni poori zindagi ko ek guarantee ke hawale mat karo.

Humein positive aur negative ke darmiyan nahi, umeed aur haqeeqat ke darmiyan balance seekhna hai.

Apne Liye Ek Doosra Raasta Rakhna

Shayad maturity ka ek matlab ye bhi hai ke hum ek hi ending par apni poori zindagi depend na karein.

Agar plan A na ho, to iska matlab ye nahi ke zindagi khatam.

Shayad plan B ho. Shayad plan C ho. Shayad koi aisa raasta ho jo humne kabhi socha hi na ho.

Aur kabhi kabhi woh naya raasta us raaste se bilkul different hota hai jise hum apni final destination samajh rahe thay.

Isi liye future ko ek fixed picture ki tarah dekhna zaroori nahi.

Hum direction choose kar sakte hain. Lekin har turn control nahi kar sakte.

Raasta badal jana, manzil ka khatam ho jana nahi hota.

Khud Se Dobara Milna

Jab hum kisi plan, kisi expectation, kisi person, ya kisi image ke tootne se guzarte hain, to kabhi kabhi ek aur cheez toot ti hai: humari apne baare mein banayi hui image.

"Main ye kar sakta hun." "Main aisa kabhi nahi karunga." "Mere saath ye nahi hoga." "Main sab handle kar lunga."

Aur jab reality in sentences ko challenge karti hai, to hum sirf situation ko nahi dekh rahe hote. Hum khud ko bhi dobara dekh rahe hote hain.

Shayad hum utne strong nahi thay jitna sochte thay. Shayad hum utne weak bhi nahi thay jitna us waqt mehsoos hua.

Shayad strength ka matlab kabhi ye tha hi nahi ke tumhein kuch feel na ho.

Shayad strong hona ye hai ke tum feel karne ke baad bhi apni zindagi ko bilkul khatam samajhne se inkaar kar do.

Sab Kuch Theek Hone Se Pehle

Hum aksar kehte hain: "Sab theek ho jayega."

Lekin shayad zindagi ka honest version ye hai:

"Sab kuch shayad theek na ho. Lekin main dekhunga ke jo mere control mein hai, uske saath main kya kar sakta hun."

Ye sentence kam beautiful hai. Lekin zyada sach hai.

Kyunki ismein guarantee nahi. Ismein ek choice hai.

Tum ye choose nahi kar sakte ke duniya tumhare saath kya karegi. Lekin tum ye seekh sakte ho ke uske baad tum kya karoge.

Tum decide nahi kar sakte ke kaun tumhein samjhega. Lekin tum apni feelings ko ignore karna band kar sakte ho.

Tum har result control nahi kar sakte. Lekin tum apni preparation control kar sakte ho.

Tum har change rok nahi sakte. Lekin tum apne aap ko change ke baad dobara build kar sakte ho.

Shayad "Ab Kya?" ka koi ek answer nahi hota.

Kabhi answer hota hai: ruk jao.

Kabhi: dobara shuru karo.

Kabhi: kuch cheezein chhor do.

Kabhi: apni baat keh do.

Aur kabhi: kuch mat karo... bas pehli baar khud se sach bolo.

Kyunki haqeeqat ke baad zindagi foran khoobsurat nahi hoti. Kabhi woh sirf khaali si lagti hai.

Aur jab purani duniya toot jaye, aur nayi duniya abhi bani na ho...

wahan se shuru hota hai — Khalipan.
