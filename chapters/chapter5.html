CHAPTER 05

Dhund

Kabhi kabhi haqeeqat saamne aa jaane ke baad sab kuch clear nahi hota.

Humein lagta hai ke jab sach pata chal jayega, to shayad agla qadam bhi samajh aa jayega. Lekin aisa zaroori nahi.

Kabhi purana raasta khatam ho jata hai, magar naya raasta nazar nahi aata.

Aur isi beech jo kuch hota hai... usay shayad Dhund kehte hain.

Dhund mein cheezein hoti hain, bas door se saaf nazar nahi aatin. Raasta hota hai, lekin uski direction samajh nahi aati. Manzil shayad wahi hoti hai, magar us tak pohanchne ka tareeqa nazar nahi aata.

Aur sab se ajeeb baat? Dhund mein hum aksar ye samajhne lagte hain ke shayad hum hi galat jagah kharay hain.

Jab Kuch Samajh Na Aaye

"Ab kya karna hai?"

Ye sawal haqeeqat ke baad aksar saamne aata hai.

Pehle humare paas plans hote hain. Phir koi cheez badal jaati hai. Plan toot jata hai. Aur achanak humare paas sirf ek khaali sa space reh jata hai.

Degree ke baad kya? Kisi decision ke baad kya? Kisi dosti ke badalne ke baad kya? Kisi expectation ke tootne ke baad kya? Jab woh cheez na mile jiske liye itna socha tha, to phir kis direction mein chalna hai?

Har sawal ka jawab foran mil jaye, zindagi shayad itni mushkil na hoti.

Lekin zindagi aksar jawab se pehle sawal de deti hai.

"Har confusion ka matlab ye nahi hota ke tum lost ho. Kabhi kabhi tum bas purani direction se nikal chuke hote ho."

Sab Aage Barh Rahe Hain

Dhund mein ek aur cheez bohot gehri hoti hai — doosron ko dekhna.

Hum dekhte hain ke koi apni degree complete kar raha hai, koi job kar raha hai, koi apna business start kar raha hai, koi apni life ke plans bana raha hai.

Aur phir hum apni taraf dekhte hain.

"Main kahan hoon?"

"Main abhi tak yahan kyun hoon?"

"Sab mujh se aage kaise nikal gaye?"

Shayad sab se dangerous comparison ye nahi ke hum doosron ko apne se behtar samajhte hain.

Shayad asli problem ye hai ke hum doosron ki visible life ko apni invisible struggle se compare karte hain.

Humein kisi ka result nazar aata hai, uske peeche ke saal nahi.

Humein kisi ki success nazar aati hai, uske doubts nahi.

Humein kisi ka confidence nazar aata hai, uske woh din nahi jab usay khud nahi pata tha ke woh kya kar raha hai.

Hum aksar apni kahani ke beech ko, kisi aur ki kahani ke end se compare karte hain.

Galat Faisla Karne Ka Dar

Dhund sirf direction ki kami nahi hoti. Kabhi kabhi ye decision lene ka dar bhi hoti hai.

Jab hum ek baar reality se takra chuke hote hain, to phir har agla qadam lene se pehle hum rukne lagte hain.

"Agar ye bhi galat ho gaya to?"

"Agar maine phir se galat choose kar liya to?"

"Agar main wapas wahi jagah aa gaya to?"

Aur phir hum decision nahi lete.

Hum wait karte hain.

Itna wait karte hain ke kabhi kabhi decision na lena bhi ek decision ban jata hai.

Humein lagta hai ke certainty mil jayegi, phir hum chalenge.

Lekin zindagi aksar certainty nahi deti. Sirf ek chhota sa agla qadam deti hai.

Aur shayad humein poora raasta dekhne ki zaroorat bhi nahi hoti.

Kabhi kabhi bas itna dekhna kaafi hota hai ke agla qadam kahan rakhna hai.

"Mujhe Nahi Pata"

Humne "mujhe nahi pata" ko aksar weakness samjha hai.

Jaise har waqt humein pata hona chahiye ke hum kya kar rahe hain, kahan ja rahe hain, aur kyun ja rahe hain.

Lekin shayad kuch stages aisi hoti hain jahan "mujhe nahi pata" sab se honest jawab hota hai.

"Mujhe nahi pata ke agla saal kaisa hoga."

"Mujhe nahi pata ke main kis direction mein jaunga."

"Mujhe nahi pata ke jo decision main le raha hoon, woh sahi hoga ya nahi."

Aur in sab ke bawajood... main agla qadam le sakta hoon.

Kyunki "mujhe nahi pata" ka matlab hamesha "main nahi kar sakta" nahi hota.

"Na jaanna aur na kar pana, dono ek cheez nahi hain."

Dhund Mein Rukna

Kabhi kabhi hum sochte hain ke rukna matlab peeche reh jana.

Lekin har rukna failure nahi hota.

Kuch rukne zaroori hote hain. Taake hum samajh saken ke hum jis direction mein itni der se bhaag rahe the, woh waqai humari thi bhi ya nahi.

Har cheez ka jawab usi waqt nahi milta jab hum uska sawal poochte hain.

Kuch answers waqt ke saath samajh aate hain. Kuch experiences ke baad. Kuch galtiyon ke baad. Aur kuch tab, jab hum khud pehle jaise nahi rehte.

Isliye agar aaj tumhe apni direction clear nahi lag rahi, to zaroori nahi ke tum kahin fail ho rahe ho.

Ho sakta hai tum bas ek purani zindagi aur nayi zindagi ke darmiyan kharay ho.

Chhote Qadam

Jab poora raasta nazar na aaye, to poore raaste ka faisla karna zaroori nahi.

Bas agla qadam.

Aaj kya karna hai? Kal kya karna hai? Kis cheez ko seekhna hai? Kis aadat ko chhorna hai? Kis cheez ko dobara try karna hai? Kis jagah "nahi" kehna hai? Kis jagah "haan" kehna hai?

Zindagi aksar inhi chhoti cheezon se dobara shape leti hai.

Humein lagta hai ke ek din achanak sab clear ho jayega. Lekin aksar clarity chalne se aati hai, kharay rehne se nahi.

Kabhi raasta pehle nazar nahi aata. Kabhi chalne ke baad nazar aata hai.

Har Cheez Ka Wapas Aana Zaroori Nahi

Dhund mein ek aur confusion hoti hai — purani cheezon ko wapas lane ki koshish.

Hum sochte hain: agar sab kuch pehle jaisa ho jaye, to shayad hum bhi theek ho jayenge.

Lekin kuch cheezein pehle jaisi hone ke liye nahi hotin.

Kuch log sirf ek phase tak saath hote hain. Kuch plans sirf ek waqt ke liye sahi hote hain. Kuch versions humare khud ke bhi sirf ek particular waqt ke liye hote hain.

Har purani cheez ko wapas paana zaroori nahi hota.

Kabhi kabhi zaroori ye hota hai ke hum maan lein: jo tha, woh tha. Ab jo hai, usay samajhna hai.

Dhund Mein Khud Ko Na Khona

Jab direction clear na ho, sab se pehle hum khud par doubt karte hain.

"Shayad main enough nahi hoon."

"Shayad mujh mein capability nahi."

"Shayad mere liye cheezein nahi bani."

Lekin har delay ko weakness ka naam dena bhi ek tarah ki khushfehmi hai — bas is baar negative khushfehmi.

Kyunki hum bina poori kahani jaane apne baare mein final decision kar lete hain.

Ek bad result ko poori zindagi ka result samajh lete hain. Ek galat decision ko apni personality samajh lete hain. Ek difficult phase ko apna permanent future samajh lete hain.

Lekin ek phase tumhari poori kahani nahi hota.

Aaj confused hona, hamesha confused rehna nahi hota.

Aaj rukna, hamesha ruk jana nahi hota.

Aur aaj na jaanna, kal bhi na jaanna nahi hota.

Shayad abhi humein poora raasta nazar nahi aa raha.

Lekin shayad humein poora raasta dekhna bhi nahi tha. Shayad sirf itna samajhna tha ke dhund mein bhi qadam uthaya ja sakta hai.

Aur jab hum ek qadam uthate hain, phir doosra... to jo cheez door se sirf dhund lag rahi thi, woh dheere dheere direction banne lagti hai.

Ho sakta hai raasta pehle se maujood ho. Bas humein usay dekhne ke liye chalna ho.

Raasta.
